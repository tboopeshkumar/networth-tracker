# Fund house icons

Small icons shown next to funds in the app, to recognise each fund house at a glance.
They are the fund houses' own marks (trademarks of their owners), resized to 128 px.

Most were taken from each fund house's website icon, or Google's copy of it where the
site blocked automated downloads. `sbi.png` is the SBI Mutual Fund mark as shown on
ET Money's fund house page, since SBI's own site icon was too small to use.

`uti.png` is adapted (resized) from [UTI logos.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UTI_logos.png)
by Zubin.nanavati, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/);
this adaptation is shared under the same licence.
